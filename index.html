Are all the cars genuine self-drive? +

Yes, all vehicles in our fleet are 100% self-drive cars with commercial rental permits. You get total privacy and control over your itinerary without any third-party driver.

What documents do I need to rent a car? +

You need to provide: (1) A valid original Indian Driving License (minimum 1 year old), (2) Aadhaar Card or Passport for identity and address proof. You can send clear photos on WhatsApp for pre-verification.

Can I take the car outside Delhi (Outstation Trips)? +

Absolutely! Our cars are permitted for inter-state travel across Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, and beyond. State border taxes and toll charges are paid directly by the renter at toll plazas or Fastag.

How does the security deposit work? +

A modest refundable security deposit is collected before departure depending on the chosen vehicle class (e.g., Thar, Scorpio, or Brezza). Upon returning the vehicle without damages or unpaid challans, the deposit is promptly refunded via UPI / bank transfer.

Is doorstep delivery available at Delhi Airport? +

Yes, we provide doorstep delivery and pickup directly at Delhi Airport (IGI Terminal 3 and Terminal 1), New Delhi Railway Station, as well as residences and hotels throughout Delhi, Noida, and Gurugram.

What is the fuel policy? +

We operate on a transparent "Same-to-Same" fuel policy. If the vehicle is handed over with a half or full tank, you simply return it with the same level. Fuel is not bundled, allowing you to pay only for what you consume.
